WHO WE ARE

From a Small Garden to
Your Doorstep

LUCE was born from a simple belief — that flowers have the power to speak when words fall short. Founded in Kuching, Sarawak, our boutique began as a backyard passion project and blossomed into a beloved local florist.

We source only the freshest seasonal blooms, carefully handpicking every stem to ensure lasting beauty and fragrance. Each bouquet is a labour of love, crafted to mark life's most meaningful moments.

From intimate everyday surprises to grand wedding floral installations, LUCE brings the same warmth and artistry to every single order.
